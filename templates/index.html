The Six System Layers

An engineered modular stack spanning flight missions to spatial decision loops.

01. MISSION LAYER

Coverage & Route Planning

Converts survey boundaries, altitudes, camera specs, and overlap thresholds into executable MAVLink / PX4 waypoints.

02. AERIAL DATA LAYER

Synchronized Telemetry

Microsecond time-aligned visual frames paired with GPS coordinates, barometric altitude, velocity vectors, and attitude angles.

03. CONNECTIVITY LAYER

Edge to Ground Pipeline

Hardware partitioning where the UAV senses and transmits, while the ground workstation executes neural ML inference.

04. INTELLIGENCE LAYER

Vision & Neural Depth

ORB epipolar camera motion recovery combined with Depth Anything V2 monocular affine depth inference.

05. SPATIAL LAYER

Fused 3D Geometry

Multi-view voxel point cloud fusion, Screened Poisson surface reconstruction, and radiance texture projection.

06. AUTONOMOUS EXPLORATION

Next-Best-View Planning

Analyzes coverage density and occlusion uncertainty to autonomously command the drone to explore unobserved regions.
