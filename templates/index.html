System Architecture

The Closed-Loop Exploration Cycle

Minab connects computer vision directly to flight planning. The reconstructed spatial model does not merely store geometry; it evaluates reconstruction completeness and determines where the UAV must travel next.

01 / PERCEPTION

Visual Acquisition

The UAV conducts an orbital flight path around the region of interest, recording continuous video to capture parallax across overlapping visual keyframes.

ORB feature detection at 30 FPS
02 / TELEMETRY

Telemetry Ingestion

Time-stamped GPS fixes, barometric altitude, attitude quaternions, and optical focal parameters are synchronized through MAVLink for downstream reconstruction.

MAVLink telemetry synchronization
03 / RECONSTRUCTION

Volumetric Meshing

Depth Anything V2 infers relative monocular depth maps. Keyframes are unprojected into a unified 3D point cloud, filtered, and meshed using Open3D Screened Poisson.

Depth Anything V2 & Poisson meshing
04 / AUTONOMY

Active Replanning

The resulting mesh is inspected for occluded surfaces and reconstruction variance. Next-Best-View (NBV) trajectories are computed to dispatch follow-up inspection runs.

Next-Best-View trajectory optimization
Modules

Core Platform Modules

Engineered for aerial surveying, spatial computing, and autonomous field robotics.

Module 01

Operational Flight Deck

Real-time camera streams pass directly into the ground control deck. Operators supervise live optical feeds, attitude indicators, flight mode states, and waypoint sequences in a single unified interface.

Integrates standard MAVLink protocols for bi-directional command routing, waypoint updates, and emergency fail-safe management during autonomous flight legs.

Module 02

Volumetric Surface Reconstruction

Optical keyframes and synchronized pose matrices feed the 3D reconstruction pipeline. Monocular depth predictions from Depth Anything V2 are backprojected into Euclidean space.

Voxel grid filtering and statistical outlier removal condition the dense point cloud before Open3D Screened Poisson meshing generates a continuous watertight triangle mesh ready for export.

Module 03

Spatial Measurement Workstation

Browser-based 3D workspace powered by Three.js. Engineers and surveyors can orbit reconstructed terrain, inspect camera trajectory arcs, calculate point-to-point Euclidean distances, and crop bounding volumes.

Exports models in standard geometry formats including OBJ, PLY, and camera trajectory JSON coordinates for external CAD and GIS integration.

Module 04

Geospatial Digital Twin Repository

Catalog and manage high-resolution photogrammetry datasets, digital surface models, and elevation layers in a centralized geospatial repository.

Filter spatial assets by geographic coordinates, capture resolution, polygon count, and sensor payload. Direct previews and access control enable multi-team collaboration.
